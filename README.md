## About

I'm a **Senior Data Analyst at Augmedix Bangladesh**, focused on automation: building the rule engines and workflows behind claims processing. Before that I worked as a **Machine Learning Engineer**, and I'm completing an **M.Sc. in CSE at the University of Dhaka**. I care about systems that hold up in production, not just in a notebook.

- **2+ years** of industry experience
- **8 research publications**: Springer, Elsevier, IEEE and arXiv
- **200 active rules** built, covering **$16M** in claims
- **Robi Datathon 3.0** national finalist · **MVP Award**, Augmedix 2025
- 👯 Open to collaboration on **computer vision and NLP** projects

---

## What I work on

- ⚙️ **Now: claims automation at Augmedix.** Rule engines that catch denials before they happen, an automated denial-resubmission pipeline, and a workflow that categorizes rejections on its own, running across 15 sites.
- 🤖 **Before: ML engineering at Anwar Technologies.** OCR pipelines for document page-number detection, document parsing with Llama 3, and sales forecasting from geospatial and weather data.
- 🔬 **On the side: research.** Metaheuristic optimization, ensemble learning for healthcare prediction, and compact CNNs for real-world image classification.
